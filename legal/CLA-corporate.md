*Draft for legal review. The corporate counterpart of the Individual CLA: an employer signs once and names the employees who may contribute on its behalf.*

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Uderia ("the Project"), whose copyright is held by Rainer Geissendoerfer ("the Owner"). This Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("Agreement") records the terms under which the Corporation named below ("You") contributes, and allows the employees You designate to submit Contributions on Your behalf.

"You" means the legal entity identified below and all other entities that control, are controlled by, or are under common control with that entity. "Control" means direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty percent of the outstanding shares, or beneficial ownership of the entity.

"Contribution" means any original work of authorship, including any modifications or additions to an existing work, that is intentionally submitted by You to the Project for inclusion in it, in any form. "Submit" has the meaning given in the Individual CLA.

You represent that You are legally entitled to grant the above licences; that each employee designated on the schedule below is authorised to submit Contributions on Your behalf; that each Contribution is Your original creation or is submitted with complete details of its source and of any licence or other restriction of which You are aware; and that You will notify the Owner of any change to the designated employees or of any fact that would make these representations inaccurate.

The Owner is not obliged to include a Contribution in the Project. Unless required by law or agreed in writing, You provide Contributions **as is**, without warranties or conditions of any kind, express or implied.
