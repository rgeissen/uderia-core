Trust Circle

The structural change

Markers on a band

Eight separate boxes read as eight separate things. A continuous arc reads as one instrument with eight positions — and it is literally the same geometry as the small dial, so the two are recognisably one family.

The band carries state

Weight before colour

Arc weight rises with attention — hairline dashed when nothing is owed, heaviest at needs you now. Legible before colour is read, and unchanged for a colourblind reader.

The tick, translated

Here, it means an act is owed

On the Live Status dial a tick means “detail behind this”. Every station here has detail, so the tick earns its keep differently: it marks the stations where something is owed — sign, attest, review.

Kept, not replaced

The star, membrane and loop stay

They carry real meaning at this altitude — the subject, the boundary, and the closure. Reduction is for decoration, not for content: the reading, the two attributes and the acts are untouched.
