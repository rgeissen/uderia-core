The affordance

Ticks mark what is engraved

A station carries a tick only when opening it tells you something the dial cannot. Stations with nothing behind them stay hairline and unticked, so the click always pays.

The centre

Left empty on purpose

The Trust Circle's centre holds the agent. This dial holds one answer, so its centre holds only a registration mark. Identity sits in the header, where it can't be mistaken for a reading.

Live

Pending is not absent

Mounted at turn start with every station unresolved, the dial fills in as each gate reports. A dotted segment means not yet; a dashed one means nothing is owed. Try “mid-turn”.
